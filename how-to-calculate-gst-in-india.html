What Is GST?

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax. It is an indirect tax introduced in India on 1 July 2017. It replaced several earlier indirect taxes and brought many goods and services under a unified taxation system.

GST is generally calculated as a percentage of the taxable value of a product or service.

Example:

Product price: ₹1,000

GST rate: 18%

GST amount: ₹180

Final price: ₹1,180

GST Calculation Formula

The basic GST calculation formula helps you find the tax amount and final price of a product or service.

GST Amount = Original Price × GST Rate ÷ 100

Final Price = Original Price + GST Amount

Example of GST Calculation

Suppose a product costs ₹5,000 and the applicable GST rate is 18%.

GST = 5,000 × 18 ÷ 100
GST = ₹900

Final Price = ₹5,000 + ₹900
Final Price = ₹5,900

How to Calculate GST on an Amount

Follow these simple steps to calculate GST manually:

  1. Identify the original taxable amount.
  2. Find the applicable GST percentage.
  3. Multiply the amount by the GST rate.
  4. Divide the result by 100.
  5. Add the GST amount to the original price.

How to Calculate CGST and SGST

When a taxable supply takes place within the same state, GST is generally divided into two components:

  • CGST: Central Goods and Services Tax.
  • SGST: State Goods and Services Tax.

For example, if the applicable GST rate is 18%, it is generally divided equally into 9% CGST and 9% SGST.

Taxable value: ₹10,000
CGST rate: 9% = ₹900
SGST rate: 9% = ₹900
Total GST: ₹1,800
Final Invoice Amount: ₹11,800

How to Calculate IGST

IGST stands for Integrated Goods and Services Tax. It generally applies to interstate supplies of goods and services.

Taxable amount: ₹10,000
IGST rate: 18%
IGST amount: ₹1,800
Total amount: ₹11,800

Common GST Rates in India

GST rates depend on the classification of goods and services. Common rate categories include:

GST Rate Description
0% Nil-rated supplies
5% Certain eligible goods and services
12% Certain goods and services
18% Many goods and services
40% Certain specified supplies under the revised GST rate structure

Always verify the applicable GST rate using the latest official notifications and product classification before preparing an invoice.

How to Calculate GST From an Inclusive Amount

Sometimes a product price already includes GST. In that situation, you need to extract the GST component from the final amount.

GST Amount = Inclusive Price × GST Rate ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

Example: Remove 18% GST

GST-inclusive price: ₹1,180
GST rate: 18%

GST = 1,180 × 18 ÷ 118
GST = ₹180

Original price = 1,180 − 180
Original Price = ₹1,000

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the formula for calculating GST?

GST Amount = Taxable Value × GST Rate ÷ 100. Add the GST amount to the taxable value to get the final price.

2. How do I calculate 18% GST on ₹1,000?

Multiply ₹1,000 by 18 and divide by 100. The GST amount is ₹180, making the final price ₹1,180.

3. How is GST divided between CGST and SGST?

For applicable intrastate transactions, GST is generally divided equally between CGST and SGST. An 18% rate consists of 9% CGST and 9% SGST.

4. What is the difference between CGST and IGST?

CGST is one component of GST on applicable intrastate supplies, while IGST generally applies to interstate supplies.

5. Can I calculate GST from the final price?

Yes. Use this formula: GST = Inclusive Amount × GST Rate ÷ (100 + GST Rate).
