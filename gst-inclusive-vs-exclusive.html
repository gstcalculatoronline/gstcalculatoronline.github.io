GST Inclusive vs Exclusive – Difference, Formula & Calculation Examples

When purchasing products or services in India, you may see prices displayed with or without Goods and Services Tax (GST). These are known as GST-inclusive and GST-exclusive prices.

Understanding the difference between GST inclusive and exclusive amounts is important for customers, retailers, business owners, freelancers and accountants.

In this guide, you will learn the difference, calculation formulas and practical examples to calculate GST accurately.

What Is GST Exclusive Price?

A GST-exclusive price is the original price of a product or service before adding GST. The applicable GST amount is calculated separately and added to the original price.

For example:

  • Product price: ₹1,000
  • GST rate: 18%
  • GST amount: ₹180
  • Final price: ₹1,180

Here, ₹1,000 is the GST-exclusive price because GST has not yet been added.

What Is GST Inclusive Price?

A GST-inclusive price is the final price of a product or service after GST has already been included. The displayed amount contains both the original taxable value and the GST component.

For example:

  • Original price: ₹1,000
  • GST amount: ₹180
  • GST-inclusive price: ₹1,180

Here, ₹1,180 is the GST-inclusive price because it already includes 18% GST.

GST Exclusive Calculation Formula

To calculate GST on an exclusive price, multiply the original taxable amount by the applicable GST percentage and divide by 100.

GST Amount = Original Price × GST Rate ÷ 100

Inclusive Price = Original Price + GST Amount

Example of GST Exclusive Calculation

Suppose a product costs ₹5,000 before GST and the applicable GST rate is 18%.

Original Price = ₹5,000
GST Rate = 18%

GST = 5,000 × 18 ÷ 100
GST = ₹900

Final Price = 5,000 + 900
GST Inclusive Price = ₹5,900

GST Inclusive Calculation Formula

If the displayed amount already includes GST, you need to extract the GST component to find the original taxable value.

GST Amount = Inclusive Price × GST Rate ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

Original Price = Inclusive Price × 100 ÷ (100 + GST Rate)

Example of GST Inclusive Calculation

Suppose a product costs ₹5,900, including 18% GST. Let's calculate the original price and GST amount.

Inclusive Price = ₹5,900
GST Rate = 18%

GST = 5,900 × 18 ÷ 118
GST = ₹900

Original Price = 5,900 − 900
GST Exclusive Price = ₹5,000

How to Remove GST From an Amount

If you receive an invoice or see a product price that already includes GST, you can calculate the original taxable value by removing the GST component.

  1. Enter the GST-inclusive amount.
  2. Identify the applicable GST percentage.
  3. Divide the amount by 100 plus the GST rate.
  4. Multiply by 100 to find the original taxable value.
  5. Subtract the taxable value from the inclusive amount to find GST.

Example: Remove 18% GST From ₹2,360

Inclusive Amount = ₹2,360
GST Rate = 18%

Taxable Value = 2,360 × 100 ÷ 118
Taxable Value = ₹2,000

GST Amount = 2,360 − 2,000
GST Amount = ₹360

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the difference between GST inclusive and exclusive?

GST inclusive means the price already contains GST, whereas GST exclusive means GST has not yet been added to the original price.

2. How do I calculate GST inclusive price?

Multiply the original price by the GST rate divided by 100. Then add the GST amount to the original price.

3. How do I remove 18% GST from an amount?

Divide the GST-inclusive amount by 118 and multiply by 100 to calculate the original taxable value. Subtract that value from the inclusive amount to find GST.

4. Does GST inclusive mean tax is already paid?

It means GST is included in the displayed price. Whether it has been properly charged, invoiced and paid to the government depends on the actual transaction and tax compliance.

5. Is GST calculated on the inclusive or exclusive price?

GST is generally calculated on the applicable taxable value. If the displayed amount already includes GST, use the inclusive formula to extract the tax component.
